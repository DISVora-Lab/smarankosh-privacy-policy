Our approach to privacy

SmaranKosh is designed to keep your active password vault encrypted on your Android device. DISVora Lab does not offer cloud synchronization of vault contents. Google Play handles purchases and related licensing services.

Privacy inquiries: smarankosh.support@gmail.com

01 Who We Are and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SmaranKosh, developed and published by DISVora Lab ("DISVora Lab", "we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use the SmaranKosh Android application ("SmaranKosh" or the "App").

SmaranKosh is designed to store vault data locally on your Android device. The App does not require a DISVora Lab account and does not provide DISVora Lab cloud synchronization for vault contents.

02 Core Privacy Principle

Your saved vault contents are intended to remain on your device unless you deliberately export them. DISVora Lab does not operate a server that receives or stores your SmaranKosh vault entries as part of the App's normal vault function.

03 Information Processed Locally on Your Device

Depending on what you choose to save, SmaranKosh may process locally on your device:

  • Titles or labels for saved entries.
  • Usernames, login IDs and account identifiers you enter.
  • Passwords, profile passwords and PINs you enter.
  • Website URLs and notes you enter.
  • Entry creation/update metadata needed by the App.
  • Local security configuration such as vault state, recovery state, biometric-unlock state and security lock information.

This information may be highly sensitive. SmaranKosh encrypts credential records before local persistence as described in the Security section below.

04 Information DISVora Lab Does Not Receive from the Vault

Under the App's normal operation, DISVora Lab does not receive or store on a DISVora Lab server:

  • Your Master Password.
  • Your saved vault passwords, usernames, PINs, URLs or notes.
  • Your Recovery Code or Recovery File.
  • Your fingerprint or face biometric template.
  • The contents of your exported vault files.

SmaranKosh contains no DISVora Lab cloud-vault feature, remote vault-reading feature or developer backdoor designed to let DISVora Lab retrieve your locally stored vault contents. Because DISVora Lab does not normally possess this local vault data, DISVora Lab cannot retrieve it for you if you lose it.

05 Google Play and Platform Services

SmaranKosh may use Google Play services for licensing, purchase processing, purchase restoration and related Play functionality. Google may process account, transaction, purchase-status, device/service or other information required to provide those services under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

DISVora Lab does not receive your complete payment-card information from Google Play. The App may receive purchase or entitlement information needed to determine whether licensed features are available.

06 Advertising and Analytics

SmaranKosh contains no advertising and does not use an advertising SDK to monetize your vault contents.

DISVora Lab does not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service that receives your vault contents. If a future app version adds a service that changes data collection or sharing, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated as required.

07 Biometric Information

If you enable Biometric Unlock, SmaranKosh asks Android to perform biometric authentication using the biometric enrollment already managed by the operating system. DISVora Lab and SmaranKosh do not receive, copy or store your fingerprint or facial biometric template.

08 Local File Import, Export and Recovery Files

When you choose to import or export a file, Android's document picker allows you to select the source or destination. Exported Recovery or Excel files leave SmaranKosh's private internal storage and are then controlled by the storage location and by you.

You are responsible for protecting exported Excel files, Recovery Files, Recovery Codes and any copies saved to shared storage, removable storage, computers, cloud drives or other locations.

09 Why Information Is Processed

Information is processed only as needed to provide the functions you choose to use, such as storing and retrieving encrypted vault entries, searching entries, changing security settings, performing recovery, importing/exporting data, using biometric unlock, enforcing security lockout and verifying Google Play licensing where applicable.

10 Sharing and Disclosure

DISVora Lab does not sell your vault contents and does not share vault contents with advertisers.

Under normal App operation, DISVora Lab has no technical mechanism within SmaranKosh to remotely retrieve or read your locally stored Master Password, saved vault entries, Recovery Code, Recovery File or exported files. Accordingly, DISVora Lab cannot sell, share or disclose local vault data that it does not possess.

Google or other platform providers may process information when you use Google Play or Android platform services. If DISVora Lab is legally required to disclose information that DISVora Lab actually possesses, it may do so to the extent required by applicable law.

11 Security Measures

SmaranKosh is designed with multiple security layers. These include Argon2id password derivation with a unique random salt, randomly generated 256-bit key material, AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, Android Keystore-backed protection for secure state, optional Android biometric authentication, security lockout, encrypted local persistence and screen-capture protections.

No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. Device compromise, malware, operating-system vulnerabilities, rooted devices, physical access to an unlocked phone, weak device security, disclosure of credentials or unsafe export handling may reduce protection.

12 Retention and Deletion

Vault data is stored locally on your device until you delete entries, clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or factory-reset/erase the device, subject to Android behavior and any copies you separately exported.

Because DISVora Lab does not maintain a cloud copy of your vault contents, there is no DISVora Lab cloud vault to delete. Purchase and transaction records held by Google are governed by Google's own retention and account processes.

Exported files are separate copies and remain wherever you saved or copied them until you remove them from those locations.

13 Your Choices and Privacy Rights

For vault contents stored only on your device, DISVora Lab cannot access, correct or delete information it does not possess. You control that local data through SmaranKosh and Android device controls.

If you have a privacy question about information that DISVora Lab actually receives through a support communication or through Google Play-related records available to DISVora Lab, you may contact smarankosh.support@gmail.com. Do not send any Master Password, Recovery Code, Recovery File, saved credential or exported vault file.

Requests concerning information controlled by Google should be directed to Google through the applicable Google services and privacy controls.

14 International Users

SmaranKosh may be offered in multiple countries. Privacy and consumer rights vary by location. This Privacy Policy provides a global description of the App's data practices and does not waive rights that applicable law does not permit to be waived.

15 Age Eligibility and Children

SmaranKosh is intended for users aged 18 years and over and is not directed to children. The App does not provide a DISVora Lab cloud account for storing vault contents.

If DISVora Lab becomes aware that a person under 18 has directly provided personal information to DISVora Lab through a support communication, DISVora Lab will handle that information in accordance with applicable law.

16 Changes to This Privacy Policy

DISVora Lab may update this Privacy Policy when the App, applicable services or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when the policy changes materially, and additional notice will be provided where required by law or platform policy.
